Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

KitLab6 is a free browser-based tool for editing PES6 kits. This page explains what data is processed when you use this website.

Your files never leave your computer. KitLab6 runs entirely in your browser. The PNG files you upload and the projects you open are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server. We do not receive them, store them, or have access to them.

No accounts, no tracking

This site does not require registration, does not ask for personal data, and does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Local storage

The tool may store small pieces of data in your browser using localStorage to remember your preferences between sessions. This information remains on your device, is not sent anywhere, and can be deleted at any time from your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted by a static hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability purposes. These logs are managed by the hosting provider, and we do not use them to identify individual users.

External links

This site contains links to third-party websites, for example partner pages or the donation page. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices; please review their own policies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

If you reside in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct or delete any personal data stored about you. Since this site does not collect personal data, in practice there is no information stored that you can request or delete, except for the preferences stored locally in your own browser.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Any changes will appear on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

To contact the author regarding this policy, please use the community or project channels linked from KitLab6.
